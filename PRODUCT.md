## Product Purpose

Present the engineer's background, projects, and skills so visitors can understand the work, inspect working demonstrations and source code, and make contact.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve access to all nine existing projects and all 42 existing skills.
- Projects should be concise and easy to explore without a second full collection list.
- Include real video demonstrations. The user selected planning new walkthroughs of the live applications.
- Use genuine technology logos and purposeful hover animation.
- Skills must not return to orbiting or automatically cycling content.
- Keep the already authorized removal of What I Build, the Journey navigation link, the Startup Journey card, and the Privacy/Terms routes.

## Brand Commitments

- The user's October 8, 2026 colors-only directive supersedes the earlier black/mint commitment across the codebase: use a light gray/white canvas, navy text, and vivid blue accents. Source tokens are defined in src/app/globals.css.
- Keep the heading format demonstrated by About Me: accent line, relevant icon, bold uppercase Poppins, and a blue final word.
- Keep typography consistent with the rest of the portfolio.
- Preserve existing layout, content, animations, and interactions during this color refresh. Authentic media and multicolor brand artwork retain their original colors; monochrome technology marks use navy against the light canvas.

## Evidence on Hand

- Project names, descriptions, technologies, live URLs, and source links: src/data/portfolio.ts.
- Skills and categories: src/data/portfolio.ts.
- Existing identity: src/app/globals.css, src/app/layout.tsx, and src/components/AboutSection.tsx.
- Nine authentic demo/site-tour videos and genuine posters are now present in public/demos; source metadata records their origins and availability limitations.

## Product Principles

- Let visitors see the actual work before asking them to read extensive descriptions.
- Keep project exploration compact and preserve every project's demo and source access.
- Prefer authentic artifacts and brand marks over decorative substitutes.
- Motion should explain focus and selection without continuously moving the content.
